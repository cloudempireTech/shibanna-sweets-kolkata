Rooted in Heritage

The Sacred Soul of Kolkata’s Mishti Culture

Nestled within the historic bylanes of Entally at 110/1/1B Dr. Lal Mohan Bhattacharya Road, Shibanna Sweets is a living ode to the timeless romance of Bengali confectionery. Anchored by the familiar chime of the Philips Bus Stop just outside our doors, the delicate aroma of slow-simmered milk, caramelized jaggery, and crushed cardamom has welcomed generations of patrons.

Yet, tradition at Shibanna is never frozen in time—it is an evolving, living craft. We honor the sacred rituals of the traditional halwai: hand-churning chhena with reverence, setting Mishti Doi in cool, unglazed terracotta, and balancing time-honored recipes with modern culinary precision.
