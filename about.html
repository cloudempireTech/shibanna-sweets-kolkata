Our History & Craft

The Art of Pure Bengali Confectionery

A heritage shaped by generations of master halwais, fresh local milk, and the cultural heartbeat of Entally, Kolkata.

A Living Ode to Old Calcutta Sweet Making

Located at 110/1/1B Dr. Lal Mohan Bhattacharya Road, near the bustling Philips Bus Stop, Shibanna Sweets has stood as a familiar sanctuary of sweetness for decades. Long before dawn illuminates the trams and streets of Entally, the crackle of wood fire and the fragrant aroma of simmering milk fill our workshop.

In Bengal, sweets are not merely food—they are emotions, blessings, and social rituals. Whether greeting family on Bijoya Dashami, celebrating a baby's Annaprashan, or sharing afternoon tea with neighbors, our recipes have accompanied generations through life's most precious milestones.

We source pure farm-fresh cow milk each dawn, curdling it into soft, moisture-rich chhena that forms the foundational canvas for our Rosogollas and Sandesh. We avoid artificial thickening agents, essences, or bulk powders. Every piece carries the genuine taste of pristine dairy and authentic craftsmanship.

Our Guiding Pillars

Four Commitments to Purity

01
Pure Chhena Daily
Freshly split cow milk curdled every morning with natural whey. Zero starch or flour fillers.
02
Unglazed Terracotta
All Mishti Doi is set in porous clay bhar crafted by Bengal potters to breathe and naturally thicken.
03
Natural Flavors
True date palm Nolen Gur in winter, crushed green cardamom pods, and Kashmiri saffron strands.
04
Morning Hing Kochuri
Freshly fried golden asafoetida puris served hot from the kadai with authentic coconut Chholar Dal.
