The Culinary Portfolio

An Atelier of Bengali Confectionery

Handcrafted daily in small unhurried batches using 100% pure cow ghee, fresh cow chhena, and traditional terracotta handis. Zero industrial shortcuts, zero artificial essences.

Heritage Signature • খাঁটি ঘিয়ে ভাজা

Pure Ghee Confections (শিবান্নর বিশুদ্ধ ঘৃতের খাবার)

As proclaimed on our founding storefront signage, our rich confections are slow-simmered and gently fried exclusively in pure cow ghee for authentic aroma and melt-in-the-mouth richness.

Slow-caramelized full-cream cow milk fermented inside unglazed, porous earthenware. The clay gently wicks away excess moisture, leaving a dense, spoonable, velvety curd with subtle smoky undertones.

Porous Terracotta Set
