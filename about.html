The Art of Pure Bengali Confectionery

A heritage shaped by generations of master halwais, fresh local cow milk, and the cultural heartbeat of Entally, Kolkata.

Living Heritage

A Living Ode to Old Calcutta Sweet Making

Located at 110/1/1B Dr. Lal Mohan Bhattacharya Road, near the bustling Philips Bus Stop, Shibanna Sweets has stood as a familiar sanctuary of sweetness for decades. Long before dawn illuminates the trams and streets of Entally, the crackle of wood fire and the fragrant aroma of simmering milk fill our workshop.

In Bengal, sweets are not merely food—they are emotions, blessings, and social rituals. Whether greeting family on Bijoya Dashami, celebrating a baby's Annaprashan, or sharing morning tea with neighbors over hot Hing Kochuri, our recipes have accompanied generations through life's most precious milestones.

We source pure farm-fresh cow milk each dawn, curdling it into soft, moisture-rich chhena that forms the foundational canvas for our Rosogollas and Sandesh. We avoid artificial thickening agents, essences, or bulk powders. Every piece carries the genuine taste of pristine dairy and authentic craftsmanship.

Neighborhood Pillar

The Gathering Counter at Philips Bus Stop

The streetfront threshold is where the neighborhood comes alive. From early morning commuters catching buses to Sealdah stopping for golden Hing Kochuris with sweet-spicy Chholar Dal, to families selecting earthen handis of Mishti Doi for Sunday lunches, our counter has remained an unhurried fixture of local hospitality.

Unlike modern commercial confectioneries that rely on factory-shipped pastes and refrigerated logistics, our sweets are turned out continuously throughout the day in small, freshly prepared batches.

Our Guiding Pillars

Four Commitments to Purity

Preserving four foundational pillars of authentic Bengali sweetmaking without compromise.

01
Pure Chhena Daily
Freshly split cow milk curdled every morning with natural whey. Zero starch or synthetic fillers.
02
Unglazed Terracotta
All Mishti Doi is set in porous clay bhar crafted by Bengal potters to breathe and naturally thicken curd.
03
Natural Ingredients
True date palm Nolen Gur in winter, whole crushed green cardamom pods, and pure saffron strands.
04
Pure Cow Ghee
As proudly declared on our original storefront, all fried delicacies use 100% pure cow ghee.
