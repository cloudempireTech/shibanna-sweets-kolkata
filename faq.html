Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fresh preparations, ordering through WhatsApp, and storage advice.

What is the shelf life of your Mishti Doi?

Because our Mishti Doi is fermented naturally in unglazed porous earthenware without artificial chemical stabilizers, it tastes freshest when consumed within 48 to 72 hours under refrigeration. Keeping it in its original clay pot maintains its signature dense texture.

How does WhatsApp ordering work?

Clicking any "Order on WhatsApp" button prepares a pre-filled enquiry with your chosen item, quantity, and preferred time. It opens WhatsApp directly on your phone or web browser so you can review and send the message. We confirm item availability and packaging immediately.

Do you undertake bulk catering orders for weddings and Pujas?

Yes, we regularly cater for weddings, receptions, Durga Puja pandals, Annaprashan, and corporate gift hampers. For bulk orders exceeding 5kg or 50 pieces, please notify us at least 24 to 48 hours in advance via WhatsApp or phone at +91 98308 95062.

What time is hot Hing Kochuri served?

Our morning breakfast Hing Kochuri is fried hot in fresh batches between 7:00 AM and 11:30 AM every day. It is accompanied by fragrant Chholar Dal with coconut and tangy chutney. Evening Singaras (samosas) are available from 4:00 PM onwards.

Are your sweets 100% vegetarian?

Yes, every single sweet and savoury confection at Shibanna Sweets is 100% vegetarian, prepared exclusively with pure dairy, sugar, jaggery, nuts, and spices.
