SHIBANNA SWEETS • 110/1/1B Dr. L. M. Bhattacharjee Road, Calcutta - 700 014

Rooted in Entally Since Inception

Anchored beside the historic Philips Bus Stop, Shibanna Sweets represents the enduring dignity of traditional neighborhood sweetmakers. Generations of commuters and neighborhood families have gathered at this counter for morning kochuri and evening earthen handi curd.
